PanelShot Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · Applies to the version 0.11.16.

PanelShot — Chat Capture & Redaction captures webpages, scrolling panels, selected regions and user-selected screens, then lets you edit, extract text, combine and export them on your device.

Data processed

The extension processes the pixels you choose to capture, optional webpage title and source URL, capture time from your device clock, imported images, annotations, optional project names and locally recognized text. These may include personal or sensitive information visible in the selected content. Do not capture information you are not authorized to use.

Local processing and storage

Capture, editing, PNG/PDF generation, OCR, ZIP packaging and cryptographic operations run locally. The OCR engine and English model are bundled with the extension. Additional language models are downloaded only when requested, from raw.githubusercontent.com (the official tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast repository) or cdn.jsdelivr.net as a fallback. These downloads expose normal request information such as the IP address and requested model to the provider, never screenshots, recognized text or source metadata. Requests omit cookies and referrers. Models are pinned and verified before saving to local IndexedDB; JavaScript and WebAssembly execution files remain bundled. Downloaded models can be used offline, removed in the language manager, and downloaded again. Clearing browser data or uninstalling may remove models. The extension has no analytics, behavior-targeted advertising, screenshot-upload endpoint or remote OCR service. Optional account sign-in is described below. Free usage allowances are recorded in this browser profile; Pro follows the account described below.

Captures are staged locally before export. Choosing edit during the notice cancels automatic export; OCR selection creates a local draft without automatic image export. Normal captures save after the choice period if editing was not selected. Temporary captures, recognized text and associated capture tiles expire after ten days without activity. Cleanup runs periodically while Chrome is running and when the extension wakes; exported files in computer folders are never removed by this cleanup. Downloaded OCR models and destination preferences are not subject to capture expiry. Browser profiles keep separate records and settings.

Language and capture preferences are stored locally. Session error diagnostics record capture stages only, not other keystrokes. Local archive signing keys are generated on-device and remain in local browser storage.

Automatic chat capture saves image blocks and progress in local IndexedDB during capture so that long or interrupted work can be exported later. Active job identifiers are kept in chrome.storage.session to recover background state; closing the browser stops capture. Long captures and their blocks follow the ten-day inactivity cleanup described above and can also be deleted manually. Automatic short captures retain blocks for smart resume under the same cleanup rule. Checkpoints include local message identifiers or text fingerprints, scroll coordinates and the source tab identifier; Telegram chat routing/account information may be retained locally to return to the same chat. Checkpoints and routing information are not included in exports. No raw message text is added to the checkpoint. Long exports omit source URLs. Partial captures are labeled incomplete.

Browser language preferences are used locally to recommend language models. Users can request multiple models in a queue. Closing the language dialog keeps downloads running; closing the editor stops unfinished downloads.

In-app announcements

Product news is enabled by default and can be turned off in Messages & updates. An explicit choice to disable it is preserved. Dismissed message identifiers and this preference are stored locally. Announcements are displayed inside the extension, not as unsolicited system notifications. They are not personalized using captured content, browsing activity or account information. This release includes a local announcement and has no remote feed URL configured. If a public feed is configured in a future release, requests will omit cookies and referrers and will not include account details, captures or subscription preferences; the hosting provider will receive normal request metadata such as the IP address. Only validated text and HTTPS links are accepted, not executable code.

Permissions and user controls

  • activeTab: temporary access to the webpage you invoke the extension on.
  • scripting: inject the selection, privacy-hiding and scroll-capture logic into that page.
  • storage: save local preferences, usage counters and membership state.
  • alarms: finish delayed local saves and clean up expired capture records.
  • clipboardWrite: copy an image or recognized text when you request it.
  • desktopCapture: show Chrome’s source picker when you request a screen or window screenshot; the selected source is used only to capture one frame.
  • downloads: save files generated by PanelShot and check the completion of those specific downloads. Other download records are not read.

Screen capture uses the browser's display-sharing chooser and requires your explicit selection. It does not request audio. Media tracks stop after the frame is captured, or when the capture page closes. The extension does not have permanent access to all websites and does not read your browser cookies, passwords or browsing history.

Exports, privacy edits and signatures

Automatic sidebar and avatar hiding uses website rules and can miss private information. Review every result. Pixelation is visual obscuring; use solid redaction where information must be removed from an exported image. Undo and the local original can retain unredacted pixels until deleted or closed.

Source metadata is optional and off by default. Query strings and URL fragments are removed from captured webpage URLs, but titles and paths can still contain identifying information. Self-signed archive packages contain the edited PNG, raster PDF, file hashes, optional source information, device times, a local signature and a verification tool. They do not contain the unedited source image. A local signature does not certify identity, webpage authenticity or trusted time.

Sharing and third parties

No capture content is automatically sent to the developer or another service. When you click an external help or email link, your browser or mail provider handles that navigation under its own policy. Choosing to send a screenshot by email or paste it elsewhere is your decision. Future cloud or trusted-timestamp features will require an updated policy before launch.

Optional account sign-in and billing

Free capture does not require an account. When you explicitly choose product sign-in, PanelShot opens crx.shiplang.com and its Google sign-in flow. The observed flow uses login.chromeextensions.workers.dev and links to the service policies at bizbrz.com. It requests your Google name, profile picture and email, not Gmail, Drive or contacts access. The service may also receive normal network request information. The extension sends its product ID, extension ID, version, browser UI language and any existing product user ID and cached expiry to that service; the extension ID is shared across installations and is not a unique computer identity. Returned product user ID, expiry and optional name/email are cached in chrome.storage.local. No screenshot, captured URL, OCR text or capture history is sent by this account bridge.

Checkout opens only when you choose to purchase. The current payment service uses PayPro Global; its billing flow may receive customer email and the selected product/order and collects payment and billing details on its own website. No payment card details are collected by PanelShot. See PayPro Global privacy information and Google privacy information. PanelShot does not impose a one-browser or one-device membership restriction. The account service returns the product membership expiry; the extension uses this cached expiry for offline access. Use Refresh membership to request a new account result. Renewal and cancellation are handled by the payment provider; cancellation does not itself remove prepaid access. Early refunds and revocations may not be reflected until a fresh account result is received. Removing local account data does not cancel a subscription or delete a provider-held account.

Deletion and contact

Delete the current capture or individual saved records from the editor. Closing other editor tabs clears their in-memory copies. Remove the extension to clear its remaining local preferences, records and signing key. The developer has no server copy of your captures to delete.

PanelShot is published by PanelEditor. For product support and privacy requests concerning the extension, contact unimeta.top@gmail.com. Requests concerning provider-held account or payment data must also be directed to the relevant account or payment provider.
